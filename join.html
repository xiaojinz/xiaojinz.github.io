JOIN THE GROUP

Let's explore the subsurface together.

We welcome motivated students and researchers with interests in computational geomechanics, multiscale modeling, mineral interfaces, and subsurface energy and environmental challenges.

Prospective PhD students

Applicants from civil, environmental, geotechnical, petroleum, earth, or materials engineering and related fields are encouraged to get in touch. Experience with molecular simulations, CFD, geomechanics, or scientific programming is helpful but not required.

To enquire, please send a single PDF containing a brief statement of research interests, CV, and academic transcripts. Formal admission and funding are subject to NTU's requirements and availability.

Email about PhD research

Research themes

  • Clay and geomaterial mechanics
  • Molecular dynamics and materials modeling
  • PFAS, nanoplastics, and engineered barriers
  • OpenFOAM and coupled multiphysics
  • Subsurface energy and geotechnics

Postdoctoral & visiting researchers

Researchers interested in collaboration, external fellowships, or visiting appointments are welcome to contact us. Please include your research interests, CV, proposed start date, and intended funding source. Availability of funded positions should be confirmed directly.
