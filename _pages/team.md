## Faculty

### Xiaojin Zheng

**Assistant Professor**, School of Civil and Environmental Engineering, Nanyang Technological University; affiliated Visiting Research Collaborator, Princeton University.

From 2021 to 2025, Dr. Zheng worked with Prof. [Ian C. Bourg](https://cee.princeton.edu/people/ian-c-bourg) at Princeton University, first as a Postdoctoral Research Associate and subsequently as an Associate Research Scholar. He received his Ph.D. in Petroleum and Geosystems Engineering from the University of Texas at Austin in 2021, supervised by Prof. D. Nicolas Espinoza.
