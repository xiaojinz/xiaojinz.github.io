## Ph.D. position

The **Environmental and Energy Geotechnics (EMERGE) Group** welcomes applications from motivated Ph.D. candidates interested in geomaterials, underground engineering, rock mechanics, environmental remediation, mineralogy, and computational research.

The [original announcement](https://sites.google.com/view/xjzheng/openings) lists **one fully funded Ph.D. position**. Please contact the PI to verify current availability and the next applicable admissions deadline.

### Qualifications for Ph.D. applicants

- Bachelor's or preferably master's degree in Civil, Environmental, Geotechnical, Petroleum Engineering, Materials Science, or a related field.
- Meet the [NTU CEE Ph.D. admission requirements](https://www.ntu.edu.sg/cee/admissions/graduate/detail/cee-phd-programme-(by-research)#admission).
- Strong motivation for research and proficiency in spoken and written English.
- Experience with LAMMPS, Materials Studio, OpenFOAM, or AI/ML tools is helpful but not required.

### How to apply

Candidates should apply through [NTU's official graduate admission system](https://www.ntu.edu.sg/admissions/graduate/radmissionguide) and are encouraged to email **[xiaojin.zheng@ntu.edu.sg](mailto:xiaojin.zheng@ntu.edu.sg)** in advance.

Please combine the following into **one PDF**: (1) a cover letter with a paragraph about your research interests, (2) your CV, and (3) undergraduate and, if applicable, graduate transcripts.

Suggested email subject: **PhD application_Name_Affiliation**. Use the same text for the PDF filename.

Scholarships: [NTU Research Scholarship](https://www.ntu.edu.sg/admissions/graduate/financialmatters/scholarships/rss#Content_C006_Col00) · [Nanyang President's Graduate Scholarship](https://www.ntu.edu.sg/admissions/graduate/financialmatters/scholarships/npgs).

## Postdoctoral researchers

The [original announcement](https://sites.google.com/view/xjzheng/openings) states that no funded postdoctoral opening was available at the time of its update. Researchers holding or applying for external funding (such as CSC) may contact the PI with a single PDF containing a cover letter (including research interests) and CV.

Relevant funding programmes to explore:
- [Lee Kuan Yew Postdoctoral Fellowship](https://www.ntu.edu.sg/research/research-careers/postdoctoral-fellowship)
- [NTU AI-for-X Postdoctoral Fellowship](https://www.ntu.edu.sg/)
- [VOYAGE Fellowship](https://www.ntu.edu.sg/)
- [Singapore–Southeast Asia Fellowship (A*STAR)](https://www.a-star.edu.sg/)

*Funding eligibility, deadlines and open/closed status should be checked on the official programme websites.*

## Visiting researchers and visiting Ph.D. students

Visitors with external funding are welcome to discuss potential collaborations. Email one PDF containing a cover letter with research interests, CV, and (for visiting Ph.D. students) academic transcripts. State whether funding has been secured or is planned, as well as the proposed visit dates and duration.

## Self-funded graduate students

Applicants considering self-funded postgraduate study may contact the PI with a cover letter, CV, and academic transcripts to discuss opportunities. All applicants must meet NTU's admissions requirements.
