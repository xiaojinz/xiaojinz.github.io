The **Environmental and Energy Geotechnics (EMERGE) Group** at [Nanyang Technological University (NTU)](https://www.ntu.edu.sg/) studies the properties and behavior of geomaterials in the Earth's subsurface, including minerals, soils, sediments, and sedimentary rocks.

Our research spans scales from nanometers to kilometers through **molecular simulations, multiphase flow modeling, and laboratory experiments**. We seek to understand mineral microstructure, interfacial interactions, and solute transport, and how nanoscale processes control the emerging mechanical and hydraulic behavior of geomaterials.

Our work addresses environmental challenges (waste containment, contaminant sequestration, coastal protection, and underground engineering), geo-energy applications (CO₂ geological storage, radioactive waste isolation, and geothermal energy), and sustainable geomaterials (clay-based functional materials, geopolymers, and waste-derived materials).
